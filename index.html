Kitco Pool fees are changing January 1, 2027

We’re introducing an annual storage fee for metals held in Kitco Pool. At the same time, we’re removing transaction fees when you buy or sell.

Here’s everything you need to know about what’s changing and how it will work.

Learn more
Same metals. More possibilities.
The changes

What’s changing

Starting January 1, 2027, two important changes will take effect for Kitco Pool.

≋

A new annual storage fee

Starting January 1, 2027, we'll introduce an annual fee to help cover the cost of securely storing and insuring the metals you hold in Pool.

There's a $15 annual minimum per Pool account, and it counts toward your storage fees for the year. It isn't added on top.

↔

Transaction fees are going away

Starting January 1, 2027, you won’t pay a transaction fee when you buy or sell Pool.

No more $14.99 online or $19.99 phone transaction fee.

Our approach

Why we’re making this change

For many years, Kitco has covered the cost of storing Pool metals without charging a separate storage fee. As those costs have increased, we’re introducing a storage fee to help offset them, based on the metals you hold and their value.

Your metals will continue to be stored securely, and we believe Pool continues to offer great value compared with other precious metals storage solutions.

Our commitment

What you can expect

◇

Secure storage

Your Pool metals will continue to be stored in secure depositories.

☂

Fully insured by Kitco

Your Pool metals will be fully insured by Kitco.

▥

The same Pool flexibility

You can continue to buy, sell and hold multiple metals in your Pool account.

The details

Annual storage rates

Starting January 1, 2027, the following annual storage rates will apply to the metals you hold in Pool:

MetalAnnual rate
Gold0.12%
Silver0.30%
Platinum0.12%
Palladium0.12%
Rhodium0.40%

Your storage fee is calculated daily based on the metals you hold and their value that day.

How it works

How your storage fee works

1

Your daily holdings

We look at the type and amount of metals you hold in Pool each day.

2

Their daily value

We use the daily market price to determine the value of your metals.

3

Your storage rate

We apply that day’s portion of the applicable annual storage rate.

4

Your storage fee accumulates

Those daily amounts add up throughout the year.

▥

Your storage fee reflects what you actually hold. Changes in metal prices, as well as any Pool metals you buy or sell, are reflected in the daily calculation.

$

How the $15 annual minimum works

The $15 minimum counts toward your storage fees. It isn't an extra fee on top.

The $15 minimum is charged per Pool account for the year. As your storage fees accumulate throughout the year, the $15 you've already paid is applied toward them.

Key dates

What to expect

1

January 1, 2027

You’ll receive an invoice for the $15 annual minimum.

The $15 counts toward your storage fees for 2027.

2

Throughout 2027

Your storage fee accumulates daily.

We’ll provide quarterly updates so you can see how your storage fees are tracking. We’ll email you when each update is available in your account.

3

January 2028

Your 2027 storage fee is finalized.

If your total storage fee for 2027 is more than the $15 minimum, you’ll receive an invoice for the remaining balance. If it’s $15 or less, there’s nothing more to pay for 2027.

Payment options

How you’ll be able to pay

▭

You’ll be able to pay your storage fees using Pool holdings, funds on your account or a credit card.

We’ll let you know when the option to choose your preferred payment method is available.

Your options

Want to talk through your options?

We understand that this change may have you thinking about what’s right for your holdings. If you’d like to explore your options, including exchanging your Pool holdings for physical precious metals, our Customer Experience team would be happy to walk you through what’s available.
